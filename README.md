# KNIGHTMANIA

## About the Game

KNIGHTMANIA is a 2D platformer inspired by the timeless charm of classic
Mario-style games.

In this adventure, players take on the role of a valiant knight trapped
in a dark and dangerous dungeon. His ultimate mission is to reach the
Eternal Flame Sword, hidden deep within the dungeon's heart.

Throughout the game, players run, jump, and climb through increasingly
difficult parkour-style levels while avoiding traps and enemies. With
tight controls, simple mechanics, and coin collection as a reward
system, KNIGHTMANIA focuses on precision platforming and quick reflexes.
